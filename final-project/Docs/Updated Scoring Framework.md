GTM SCORING METHODOLOGY   
QUESTION WEIGHTS & AUDIT FINDINGS

HOW SCORING WORKS

\- Each question is scored 1–5 by the respondent.  
\- A question's weight affects the weighted average within its pillar (Demand, Conversion, Delivery).  
\- Pillar weights: Demand 35% · Conversion 35% · Delivery 30%.  
	\-  This makes it more balanced than it was previously, but still emphasizes demand and conversion over delivery, which makes sense in a growing company setting where delivery is easiest to adjust in comparison to demand and conversion

\- The overall GTM score is a weighted rollup across pillars, mapped to recommendation bands.
