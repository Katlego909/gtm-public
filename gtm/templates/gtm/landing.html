Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start

Is this free to use?

Yes. There's no payment required to take the assessment or view your results.

How long does the assessment take?

Most people finish in about 10 minutes. It's 18 questions across three GTM pillars — Demand, Conversion, and Delivery.

What happens to my answers and any documents I upload?

Your responses and any uploaded evidence are used to generate your score and playbook, including analysis by Google's Gemini AI. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need to create an account to see my results?

No — you can complete the assessment and see your results without signing up. Creating an account lets you save your history and revisit past assessments.

Can I retake the assessment to track progress?

Yes. Assessments are unlimited, and if you're signed in you can compare past results to see what's improved over time.

How is my score calculated?

Scoring is deterministic — the same answers always produce the same score, based on fixed category weights. AI is only used afterward, to write up insights and recommendations grounded in that score.
